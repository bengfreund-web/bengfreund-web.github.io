Thank you for expressing your interest in the Gallatin Wranglers Rugby program. We’re excited that you're considering joining us!

Since launching in January 2023 with just one team, we've expanded to offer:

  • Boys Varsity & JV, Girls Varsity (ages 15–19)
  • Junior Rugby Boys + Girls (ages 13–14)
  • Youth Rugby Boys + Girls (ages 11–12)
  • Rookie Rugby Co-Ed (ages 5–10)

Rugby is a fast paced, two way, full contact evasion sport that has many opportunities collegiately, professionally and internationally. The sport is a unique blend of teamwork, camaraderie, and adrenaline that forms a life-long bond among its players. Rugby is seen as a complimentary sport to our existing sports that are played at school. We aim to make you a better all around athlete and enjoy every moment of your Rugby experience. Whether you're an experienced player or new to the game, the Gallatin Wranglers provide a supportive, fun and learning environment for all skill levels and sporting backgrounds. We understand that you may have questions, and we are more than happy to provide the information you need. Below, you'll find some essential details about our program:

1. Season Schedule

Rugby is a Spring sport, played on Saturdays, in which consists of the following key blocks (pre-season, in-season and post-season) culminating in the State Championship. We have players joining at all times during the year and due to being a young sport, we are always working with new players - so please do not feel intimidated.

Block Who When What
Block 1 Varsity Teams January – March Preseason
Block 2 Junior Teams February – March Preseason
Block 3 Youth Teams March Preseason
Block 4 All Teams 3/29/25 – 5/16/26 Competitive Season
Block 5 All Teams 5/23/26 – 5/31/26 Post Season/Playoffs

We compete with 8 clubs across Montana (Missoula, Kalispell, Butte, Bozeman, Billings, Great Falls, Hamilton, Helena), including 3 home and 3 away matches per season. All teams compete in the State Championship regardless of standing.

3. Coaching Staff

We have a dedicated team of experienced and certified coaches who are passionate about rugby and committed to helping each player reach their full potential on and off the pitch. Our coaching staff boast former international coaches and players, who have represented USA and Team USA on the world’s stage.

4. Values & Culture

Our program emphasizes fun, learning, and sportsmanship, creating an environment that promotes personal growth and character development. We want to see our kids succeed in other sports too and believe that Rugby is an exceptional spring sport to prepare players for the Fall.

5. Equipment

Player registration fees cover a tech tee, mouthguard, boot bag and a rugby ball. We will provide all match day playing attire (jersey, shorts and socks). You will need to bring your own boots/cleats (preferably soft ground).

6. Cost

The cost to play per season is $292.10 for Varsity (U19) players, $271.31 for a Youth player, $198.54 for a Junior player, and $140.33 for a Rookie player. We are the most affordable club sport there is and can help those who may need assistance.

For a U19 player, the cost is broken down as follows: USA Rugby Registration & Insurance ($51.31), Montana Youth Rugby Union registration ($100), Gallatin Wranglers Club Fee ($140).

7. Opportunities

Rugby is a varsity sport on the Men’s collegiate side with over 50 varsity programs across the country offering scholarships. Women’s Rugby is a NCAA emerging sport, with both varsity & NCAA scholarships available. Rugby 7s is an Olympic Sport (with Team USA winning Bronze at the last Olympics) and Rugby 15s has both a Men’s & Women’s professional league here in the US and the Rugby World Cup is the third most watch event. We are a truly global game.
